# Cancellation Policy Notes

A hotel is only treated as "flexible cancellation" in this system if it
allows a full refund up to 24 hours before check-in with no fee. Hotels
marked `cancellation_flexible: false` in the inventory either charge a fee
on any cancellation or are non-refundable after booking - do not recommend
these when the traveler has explicitly asked for flexible cancellation,
even if they are otherwise the best fit on price or rating.

Activities booked as day trips (Dudhsagar, scuba diving) typically require
24-48 hour notice for a refund; this system does not track per-activity
cancellation terms and should not claim activities are freely cancellable.
