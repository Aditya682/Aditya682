# Goa Trip Planning Tips

- A 5-day trip comfortably supports 1 full sightseeing/day-trip day, 1
  adventure/water-sports slot, and the remaining days as unscheduled beach
  time - don't over-pack the itinerary with back-to-back activities.
- Budget travelers should prefer North Goa beach areas (Candolim, Morjim,
  Colva) over the most crowded strips (Baga, Calangute) for better
  price-to-quality ratio at similar beachfront proximity.
- For couples/small groups (2 travelers), a mid-range beachfront hotel with
  breakfast included is usually better value than a budget non-beachfront
  hotel plus separate meal costs.
- Always keep a misc/buffer allocation (transport, meals outside the hotel,
  incidentals) separate from the hotel + activities budget - a plan that
  spends 100% of the stated budget on hotel and activities alone is not
  realistic.
