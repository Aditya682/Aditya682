# Goa Areas Guide

## North Goa (Baga, Calangute, Candolim, Anjuna, Vagator, Morjim)
Busier, more nightlife and water sports. Candolim and Anjuna have a calmer,
more upscale beach strip than Baga/Calangute, which are the most crowded.
Morjim is quieter and popular with long-stay travelers. Good base for
travelers who want beach access plus easy day trips to forts and markets.

## South Goa (Palolem, Colva, Margao)
Quieter, fewer crowds, better for travelers who want a relaxed beach stay
without heavy nightlife. Palolem is consistently rated the most scenic South
Goa beach. Margao is inland and functions as the commercial/transit hub, not
a beach base.

## Panjim (Capital)
Not a beach town. Useful as a base only if the traveler explicitly wants
heritage/city sightseeing over beach time.

## Distances (approximate, by road)
- North Goa beaches to Old Goa churches: 30-45 min
- North Goa beaches to Dudhsagar Falls: 2-2.5 hours (full-day trip)
- South Goa beaches to Dudhsagar Falls: 1.5-2 hours
- North Goa to South Goa beach areas: 1-1.5 hours
